# Country rules: Australia

Apply only to the actual institution and programme. Verify current official policies and funding terms on the day of research.

### Australia

- Examine ARC, NHMRC and institution-specific research funds separately from doctoral scholarships.
- The **Research Training Program (RTP)** is administered by universities. Verify tuition-fee offsets, stipends, and allowances **individually**, including international eligibility, application route, and duration. One component does not guarantee all three.
- Confirm higher degree by research (HDR) admission, Confirmation of Candidature, supervision team/panel **where required**, progress reviews, and written award terms. PI interest is not admission or scholarship approval. The university determines RTP selection and offers; domestic and international eligibility may both exist, but **RTP component(s) are not automatic** (https://www.education.gov.au/research-block-grants/research-training-program/research-training-program-frequently-asked-questions-students). Check who actually provides daily technical training and how main/associate supervisors resolve conflicting advice. Do not assert identical supervision panel minimums at all universities.
