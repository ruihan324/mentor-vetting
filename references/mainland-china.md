# Country and region rules: Mainland China

Apply only to institutions in Mainland China, including university and Chinese Academy of Sciences contexts. Do not infer graduate-recruitment authority from a professor title or institution status.

## Mandatory checks
- Verify current institution and its former names; distinguish employment from degree-awarding institution.
- Confirm actual master's/doctoral supervisor listings, current recruitment notices, and joint-supervision agreements; absence of a listing is not proof of ineligibility.
- Verify academy membership, high-level talent programme designations, grants, and dates against issuing institutions.
- Research NSFC, university grants and provincial awards; label horizontal/industry versus vertical/public funding while avoiding assumptions about student labor. NSFC public KD can provide project/close-out records, but the NSFC FAQ says some applicant/participant histories require the researcher or host institution to log in; **do not invent a complete public grant history**. Source: https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/2961/2962/3648/cx.html
- Verify Chinese-language theses through the university, CNKI, or Wanfang as accessible; theses alone cannot yield dropout/delay rates, which require a documented entering cohort and outcomes. Acknowledgments cannot establish unfair authorship or a particular mentoring style.
- Check student first/co-first contribution and actual graduate placements where records permit.
- Check the Ministry of Education 2020 supervisor conduct guidelines as a **normative baseline** (fair recruitment, guidance, graduation, research integrity, funding and conduct): https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A22/s7065/202011/t20201111_499442.html . For named individuals distinguish allegations, investigation and *final official findings*; do not assume policy non-compliance from online rumors.
- Anonymous comments are low-strength leads, never direct proof of misconduct. No "dangerous professor demographic", mind-reading of polite comments, or assumptions that industrial grants cause student exploitation.

Use the source directory in `verification.md` and the eight dimensions in `evaluation.md`.
