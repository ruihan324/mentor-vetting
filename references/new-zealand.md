# Country rules: New Zealand

Apply only to the actual institution and programme. Verify current official policies and funding terms on the day of research.

### New Zealand

- Examine MBIE, Royal Society Te Apārangi/Marsden, HRC, university, and industry funds; **MBIE is not the entire funding landscape**.
- Separately examine university doctoral scholarships, international doctoral tuition eligibility and residence conditions, award duration, research consumables, and conference costs. PI grants are not the same as university-funded student awards.
- Distinguish **supervisor willingness**, **institutional admission eligibility**, and **approved funding**. Verify formal supervision, doctoral confirmation/progression, and arrangements should a supervisor depart. For the University of Auckland, the published Doctoral Supervision Policy provides for a main, joint or co-supervisor/adviser structure and expects full-time doctoral candidates to meet at least monthly with one or more supervisor/s (https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/about-us/about-the-university/policy-hub/research-innovation/doctoral-study/supervising/doctoral-supervision-policy-procedures.html). This is an **institution/programme-specific rule**, not a nationwide two-week meeting guarantee. For international Auckland PhD students, domestic-equivalent PhD tuition requires the specified New Zealand residence conditions; confirm the actual degree and year (https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/fees-and-money-matters/tuition-fees/doctoral-fees.html).
