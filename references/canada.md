# Country rules: Canada

Apply only to the actual institution and programme. Verify current official policies and funding terms on the day of research.

### Canada

- Examine NSERC, CIHR, SSHRC and institutional grants separately from guaranteed programme support, TA/RA components, and individual awards.
- If programmes such as **CGRS D** are relevant, verify their **current** name, international eligibility, registration requirements, institutional quotas, and award stacking. Do not assume a national scholarship is open to every incoming applicant.
- Check qualifying exams, international tuition, committed funding duration, and field/programme-specific completion times.
