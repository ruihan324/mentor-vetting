# Country rules: United Kingdom

Apply only to the actual institution and programme. Verify current official policies and funding terms on the day of research.

### United Kingdom

- Distinguish university admission, specific advertised research project, and **studentship**. UKRI funding is commonly administered through eligible research organizations and doctoral training structures; a PI grant is not automatically a personal studentship.
- Check international eligibility, international/home fee gap, maintenance support, duration, award contract, primary/secondary supervision. Confirm studentship terms from university/UKRI rather than inferring stipend or full international fee coverage from the project being described as funded (https://www.ukri.org/apply-for-funding/ukri-funded-studentships/).
- Search UKRI Gateway to Research, Wellcome, and applicable charities, plus institutional doctoral progression and grievances policy. The SGSAH **archived** AHRC-funded supervision benchmarks include 10 annual meetings, a typical three-week feedback time, and a normal six-FTE student load: https://www.sgsah.ac.uk/archive/supervisors/policies/benchmarks/ . These are **not UK-wide caps or universal entitlements**; verify the target institution, consortium, programme and current effective revision before reporting any numerical requirement.
