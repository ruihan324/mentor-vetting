# Country rules: United States

Apply only to the actual institution and programme. Verify current official policies and funding terms on the day of research.

### United States

- Determine whether the **actual programme** uses central admissions plus laboratory rotations, direct PI recruitment, or another route. Harvard BBS is an official example of first-year rotations (https://gsas.harvard.edu/policy/biological-and-biomedical-sciences), **not** evidence that all US STEM doctorates rotate. If rotations apply, check real training, structured feedback, student autonomy, group practices and funding through direct participation; do not assume rotation availability before admission. Separate programme acceptance, PI consent, TA/RA/fellowships, and first-year vs later support.
- Search NIH RePORTER, NSF Award Search and relevant DOE/USDA awards; verify PI and institutional identity. Public grant totals are not individual student funds.
- Check tenure-track/tenured/research appointment, start-up lab infrastructure, co-supervision, and formal withdrawal/transfer arrangements.
- Qualifying exams and coursework vary by programme; do not impose a universal time-to-degree threshold.
