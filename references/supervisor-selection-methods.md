# Supervisor discovery, selection and comparative decision method

Use this file for any master's, PhD, postdoctoral or supervisor-choice request. Preserve the original eight internal dimensions; this is an execution method, **not a ninth dimension or a reader-facing recap**.

## Scope-first triage

1. Identify **country, institution, faculty, degree/appointment type, admissions pathway, research subfield, and applicant's stated goals**. If unknown, do not invent.
2. Establish **non-negotiable constraints**: formal supervisor authority, route to admission, academic eligibility, tuition and living support, project feasibility, deadlines, applicable employment/visa terms. A verbal PI welcome is not an admission or funding offer.
3. Establish four *separate* objects of evidence: (a) researcher's past achievements, (b) current scientific independence and programme continuity, (c) each *present* project's rigor/feasibility/student ownership, (d) supervision quality/applicant fit. An established PI's achievements cannot fill unknown project design or student experiences.
4. Compare options only after the minimum constraints are checked. Do not give artificial aggregate scores, acceptance probabilities or unsupported global rankings.

## Discovery channels and their claim-specific limits

| Channel | Strong for | Weak for / special check |
|---|---|---|
| University/department pages and postgraduate handbooks | Appointment, supervision roles, current programme rules, publicly advertised projects | Mentoring behavior, current personal stipend balance; marketing copy may be dated |
| DOI publisher records, contribution statements and ORCID | Reported methods/discoveries, affiliations and stated contribution roles | Independent PI status without further evidence; teaching and supervisory quality |
| Funding agency individual award records | Project PI, amount *as awarded*, dates, scope | Funds remaining, award-specific international student eligibility, current funded slot |
| CNKI/Wanfang/institutional theses and alumni pages | Individual completed theses, named supervisors and documented graduates | **Cannot calculate completion, delay or dropout rates without entering cohort, exit dates and outcomes**; acknowledgments are not personnel evaluations |
| Direct student/alumnus conversations (only if actually conducted) | Described experiences of feedback, onboarding, authorship processes, workload | Not a representative census; note role, date, first-hand basis, and independent corroboration |
| Reddit, Xiaomuchong, Zhihu, video/social platforms, review sites | Questions, leads and possible patterns to check | Anonymous/reposted narratives are not misconduct findings; never claim to have visited blocked sources |
| Media, lab publicity and professional networks | Dated public announcement and verifiable career trajectory | Promotional selection, incomplete population, hearsay |

## Relative evaluation with imperfect evidence

- Use a compact comparison with **eligibility; project fit; documented scientific contributions; current resources and scope; student support; supervision team and continuity; degree duration/risk; career skills**. For each, show source-backed facts, evidence gaps and decision relevance; use the eight named peer-level report topics without adding a second checklist or scorecard.
- Scientific quality: review representative work and the person's actual contribution. Explicitly compare *like subfields and career stages*; journal labels, h-index, talent awards, tenure and age are not proxies for good supervision.
- Project: check the **question, novelty against relevant prior work, controls, methods, access to equipment/data, funded duration, candidate's responsibilities, realistic milestones, fallback design and training value**. A title or grant abstract permits at most a preliminary appraisal.
- Training: distinguish verified student outcomes, personal experiences, and unavailable cohort rates. Absence of a former student record neither proves success nor misconduct.
- Compare early-career and established PIs through actual mentoring arrangements, start-up infrastructure, grant terms, support staff, publication ownership, and continuity planning, not assumed personality or diligence.
- A postdoc comparison additionally requires employment contract, immigration/visa dependency as a *structural* issue when relevant, career independence, IP/publication rights and funded duration; not demographic profiling of lab members.

## System and country routing (verify programme-specific rules)

- **Mainland China:** graduate recruitment listings and adviser eligibility, university/academy appointments, current project announcements, NSFC verified award and public close-out reports, theses and graduates. Vertical/public vs horizontal/industry projects are **different aims**, not evidence that one is unethical or inferior. Ministry of Education 2020 supervisor conduct rules are a national normative baseline; check actual violations against final official findings, not online allegations.
- **United States:** identify whether *this programme* uses first-year laboratory rotations / programme admission or direct PI admission before prescribing whom to contact. Rotation is a valuable first-hand observation opportunity when applicable, not universal to all STEM or all USA PhDs. Verify RA/TA/fellowship package and transition among years.
- **United Kingdom:** check advertised funded project, institutional/DTP/CDT studentship rules, fee-status and international fee gap, stipend/tuition/expenses, contract duration, primary/secondary supervision. **Specific consortium benchmarks are not UK-wide law**; cite their actual scope and current version.
- **Australia:** assess university HDR admission, formal supervisor endorsement where required, confirmation/progress requirements, co-supervision/panel roles. RTP fees-offset, stipend and allowances are *separate*, competitively administered university decisions; verify international eligibility individually.
- **New Zealand:** inspect doctoral/mature research-master's policy for the *specific university*, actual supervisor accreditation and team responsibilities, scholarship and tuition eligibility. At University of Auckland, current doctoral supervision policy states a full-time candidate is expected to meet **at least monthly with one or more supervisors**; confirm current effective policy and whether candidate's programme is covered. International domestic PhD tuition has residency conditions and does not itself grant living support.
- **Canada:** preserve the existing country-specific module. Check degree programme admission, faculty approval, funding guarantees, supervisory committees and institutional requirements individually. Do not force Canadian programmes into UK/Australia/New Zealand patterns merely because they are Anglophone.

## Decision output without duplicate sections

The final CV-style document synthesizes **researcher capability**, **present project viability**, and **mentoring/training fit**, with critical uncertainty and 3–7 stakeholder-specific questions. Each fact appears once in its natural section; hypotheses or unanswered questions appear as such. Report the evidence completion level accurately. Keep the original eight-dimension coverage map *internal*.
