# Review of Supervisor-Selection-Research-Handoff.md

**Assessment date:** 2026-10-31. The uploaded WorkBuddy handoff is a *research lead*, not an automatically authoritative reference. We selectively compared material claims to the official sources below. The handoff does **not** supply stable original URLs for most anecdotes; these remain uncorroborated.

## Adopted, with safeguards

1. **Four interview targets:** PI, current students, graduates/departed students, graduate office. Adopt *specific observation questions*, no invented interview responses. Grounding: University of Toronto supervisor selection guidance and University of Auckland supervisor policy.
2. **Researcher achievement vs proposed project vs supervising performance:** keep distinct; use research significance, rigor/feasibility and team/resources from NIH peer review as a *reference*, not a scoring formula.
3. **US rotations vs direct admit:** programme-level, not national field-wide. Harvard Biological and Biomedical Sciences is a validated first-year-rotation example.
4. **UK funding/studentships:** UKRI says it funds eligible organisations rather than accepting individual students' direct applications. Check international fees and term individually.
5. **China Ministry of Education conduct guidelines:** verified 2020 national guidance on recruitment, student support, research integrity and non-academic work; a policy prohibition does **not** prove any named researcher violated it.
6. **China NSFC KD:** public end-of-project records exist, but NSFC expressly notes some individual grant participation inquiries require authenticated personnel or university access. No exhaustive public award-count claims.
7. **Australia RTP:** university selection, fee offsets, stipend, allowances and international eligibility require separate verification.
8. **New Zealand University of Auckland:** official doctoral policy supports the at-least-monthly full-time meeting expectation with *one or more* supervisors, accreditation rules and clearly assigned roles. Official fee page states residence conditions for international PhD domestic-equivalent tuition. Different universities/programmes require their own checks.
9. **Source reliability by claim:** a public university profile validates appointment; it cannot establish lab culture. Anonymous stories may generate interview questions, not accusations.
10. **Supervision continuity, training and IP/authorship:** valuable applicant checks where supported by actual project/admission context, not automatic adverse findings.

## Rejected, narrowed or not transferred into Skill

| Handoff statement/theme | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| CNKI thesis pages show a supervisor's dropout / delayed-graduation rate | **Reject.** Requires documented entry cohort and complete outcomes; public theses are a selected graduate sample. |
| 'Corresponding-author position reveals exploitation' | **Reject.** Cannot infer unfairness without author contributions and applicable conventions. |
| PI emails answered fast/late reveal character, interest or work boundaries | **Reject as diagnostic.** Timing has confounding factors; seek concrete commitments and follow-up. |
| 50/50, 80/20 or other universal advisor-team formulas; UK <=6 students | **Reject as universal.** Archived SGSAH consortium benchmark has six-FTE *normal* load; institution and current policy vary. |
| 'All Australian and NZ doctoral students have two active supervisors', fixed national cadence | **Narrow.** Verify the programme-specific official structure and roles; Auckland's current rule is at least monthly with one or more supervisors. |
| China fully controls any departing supervisor's students; foreign programmes allow students to follow the PI | **Reject as universal.** Transfer/visa/funding ownership governed by each institution and contract. |
| 'Young or senior PI is better', 'mid-career PI is ideal', 'all postdoc-heavy labs are toxic' | **Reject generalizations.** Compare actual support, resources and plans instead. |
| Demographic risk lists based on nationality, gender, ethnicity, age, lab gender distribution | **Reject unequivocally.** Use documented conduct and fair treatment, never protected traits. |
| 'Academic black-speech' phrases have single covert meanings | **Reject.** Canned phrases provide no verified mental-state inference. |
| 'PI prevents private meeting with students' mandates automatic rejection | **Narrow.** Ask why, consider privacy/availability, seek independent contacts; it is not standalone proof of abuse. |
| Single report, allegations, reposted social media, attributed unofficial sex harassment stories prove misconduct | **Reject.** Prioritize final publicly documented official actions; distinguish allegation/investigation/finding. Do not republish non-final allegations as facts. |
| Exact stipend estimates, universal IELTS levels, all NZ international doctorates domestic-fee, all UK deadlines | **Reject as universal.** Check exact institution, degree, year and international eligibility. |
| Postdoc visa dependency or industry collaborations make PI suspicious | **Narrow.** Relevant terms can affect bargaining power or IP; not a conclusion about any PI or ethnic/national cohort. |
| 'Frequent recruitment ads' means poor lab culture | **Reject.** Could reflect growth, grants or turnover; verify causes. |
| Unsupported statistics on bullying, student completion or lab-type success | **Exclude.** No verified original study and denominator in handoff. |

## Limitations and reproducibility

- Platform testimonials (Bilibili, Douyin, Zhihu, Reddit, GradCafe) were **not independently verified for this upgrade**. Their suggested interview prompts were accepted only when generally sensible and supported by other sources, not their original stories or claims about prevalence.
- Official policies can change. Confirm the *effective edition and scope* for each researcher and university at assessment time.
- The Skill does not itself contact students or certify advisor conduct. Automated tests check whether safeguards are encoded and structured claims satisfy minimum proof rules; they do not establish real-world scientific or ethical truth.
