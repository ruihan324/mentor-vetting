# Word-only report and eight topic headings

A completed vetting task delivers **one `.docx`**. PDF, slide, HTML and screenshot copies are not deliverables. A renderer may internally convert DOCX into PNG page images for visual inspection only.

The reader must see these eight *ordinary, equal-level, named* report sections. Use the English titles in the master or the one-to-one Chinese translations in `references/academic-cv-style.md`:

1. Identity and Background
2. Research Direction and Applicant Fit
3. Research Output and Scientific Capability
4. Funding and Research Projects
5. Student Supervision and Outcomes
6. Laboratory Culture and Team Practices
7. Academic Reputation and External Recognition
8. Communication and Accessibility

Never call this list "eight dimensions" in the report. Do not add a second checklist, a table recapping these topics, or repeat research achievements between sections. Keep one decisive source-backed fact in its owning section. A concise uncertainty statement is preferable to invented data. Add a short Decision and Questions section and a Sources section.

Before delivery: render all Word pages, visually inspect, run `validate_report.py` with a coverage map, audit each important citation against primary sources, and ensure filenames are safe ASCII if downloads have caused trouble.
